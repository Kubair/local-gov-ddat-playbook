# DDaT Ways of Working series

**Practical ceremony guides for multi-lane DDaT programmes**

---

## What this series is

Four guides covering the ceremonies and workflows that sit inside a DDaT programme sprint cycle. Each one is written for the person running it -- not for the person who designed the process.

They exist because ceremony guidance in most programmes is either absent or abstract. Teams end up improvising stand-ups that drift into status meetings, reviews that get passive audiences, and technical conversations that waste a Solution Architect's morning. These guides fix that at the source.

---

## Where each guide fits in the sprint

The [hub README](../README.md) has a flow diagram showing how these four guides connect across the sprint cycle. The short version: the stand-up runs every day; the gate fires when a BA needs SA input; the review closes the sprint; the guest guide shapes the review when external attendees are in the room. They form a system, not a collection.

## Conventions across all guides

**No em dashes in communication.** Guides use double hyphens for ranges and pauses -- this is intentional; it is more readable across devices and tooling.

**Role titles, not names.** Every guide refers to roles, not individuals. Swap in names as needed when you adapt them.

**Pin, do not post repeatedly.** These are reference documents. Pin them in the relevant Teams channel before the first ceremony. Do not re-post each sprint.
